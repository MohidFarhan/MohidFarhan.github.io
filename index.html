Mohid Farhan

Physicist | Cosmology, Dark Sector, and Particle Physics

Institute of Space Technology (BS Physics, 2025)

Islamabad, Pakistan

I am a President’s Gold Medal-winning physicist working at the intersection of particle physics, cosmology, and dark-sector phenomenology. My research focuses on interacting dark energy, dark matter-dark energy momentum exchange, self-interacting dark matter, extensions of the Standard Model and effects of dark sector interactions on cosmological observables, such as large scale structure, structure formation and growth, along with CMB and lensing patterns.

I have authored peer-reviewed publications in Physica Scripta and have several manuscripts under review in leading journals including JCAP, MNRAS, EPJC, and Chinese Physics C. My work has been recognized with the University of Queensland Graduate School Scholarship (UQGSs/RTP) and a Direct PhD Award from KFUPM.

I am currently an ad-hoc reviewer for Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena (Elsevier).

Beyond research, I write about fundamental physics on my blog, using intuitive explanations and visualizations to make complex concepts accessible.
