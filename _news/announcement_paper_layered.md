My first paper, *"Layered dark structure with a structuring field: a Z₄-symmetric inert doublet-singlet realization and implications for the S₈ tension"* (with Marriam Naeem), has been published in **Physica Scripta** (IOP). I served as corresponding author.

My contribution was to provide the Layered Dark Sectors with a Structuring Field (LDS-SF) framework with a particle-physics foundation through the Z₄-symmetric Inert Doublet-Singlet Model (Z₄-IDSM). I then ran the model through SARAH, SPheno, micrOMEGAs, and CLASS to compute relic densities, mass spectra, and cosmological predictions, and confronted the results with Planck CMB, BAO, and growth-rate measurements.

The self-interacting dark matter framework can bring the predicted S₈ amplitude into agreement with weak-lensing data from KiDS-1000 and DES.
