My solo-authored paper, *"Symmetry-protected momentum exchange between dark matter and dark energy,"* has been published in **Physica Scripta** (IOP).

I developed the full model from the ground up, which began from a particle-physics realization of interacting dark energy in which a radiatively stable pseudo-Nambu-Goldstone-boson dark energy sector couples to WIMP dark matter through pure momentum exchange. The symmetry structure forbids the usual renormalizable portal operators and leaves derivative interactions as the leading coupling, so energy transfer is absent at the background level while momentum exchange modifies the evolution of cosmological perturbations.

I implemented the model self-consistently in the CLASS Boltzmann solver and studied its impact on the growth of structure. The key result is that symmetry-protected, momentum-exchange-only interactions possess an intrinsic limit on their suppression of the clustering amplitude σ₈, providing a theoretically controlled benchmark for interacting dark energy scenarios.

Follow-up papers are on the way!
