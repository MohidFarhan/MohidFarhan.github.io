I've been invited by the editorial board of **Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena** (Elsevier) to serve as an ad-hoc peer reviewer.

The invitation reflects the areas I work in: cosmological perturbation theory, dark matter and dark energy interactions, interacting dark energy, and self-interacting dark matter. I'll be evaluating the mathematical formulations of a series of problems, their consistency conditions, numerical verification, and connections to observational signatures in the dark sector.

Refereeing for an Elsevier journal this early in my career is not something I expected, and I'm grateful to the editorial board for the trust. It's a good reminder that the work I've been doing on dark-sector physics is being read by people I've never met, and that the standards I hold my own research to are the same ones I'll now be applying to someone else's. I'm certainly looking forward to it!
