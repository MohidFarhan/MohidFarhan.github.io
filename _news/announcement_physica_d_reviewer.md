I've completed my first peer review for **Physica D: Nonlinear Phenomena** (Elsevier), serving as an ad-hoc reviewer for a full-length manuscript on dark-energy reconstruction.

The invitation reflects the areas I work in: cosmological perturbation theory, dark matter and dark energy interactions, interacting dark energy, and self-interacting dark matter. I evaluated the mathematical formulation of a reconstruction problem, its consistency conditions, numerical verification, and its connections to observational signatures in the dark sector.

Completing a review for an Elsevier journal this early in my career is not something I expected, and I'm grateful to the editorial board for the trust. It's a good reminder that the work I've been doing on dark-sector physics is being read by people I've never met, and that the standards I hold my own research to are the same ones I've now applied to someone else's.

This was a truly insightful experience and I learned a lot about the struggles on the other side of peer-review.
