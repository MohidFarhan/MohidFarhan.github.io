I was invited to speak at the **18th National Symposium on Frontiers in Physics**, jointly hosted by the National Centre for Physics (NCP) and Quaid-e-Azam University (QAU) in Islamabad.

I delivered a technical talk on *"Unified Frameworks for Dark Matter and Dark Energy,"* presenting results from the Three-Higgs Doublet Model (3HDM) and insights from these talks paved the way for me to explore Inert Doublet plus Singlet Model (IDSM). It was my first time presenting to the national physics community, and it was definitely nerve-racking, but the discussion afterwards on the implications of multi-Higgs models for cosmological tensions was genuinely useful for my research. I am very grateful to QAU and NCP for giving me such a stellar opportunity.
